## Why

Most of my Wrike work sits on a few heavy surfaces: Reporting, Table View, Budgeting, time tracking, request forms and the Automation Engine. Table View's data-grid was the one that could not keep up. At 100k rows it took 10 seconds to render, and the same performance incidents kept returning.

## What changed

- Re-architected the data-grid to render 100k+ rows
- Render time cut by 70%, from 10s to 3s
- The recurring performance incidents stopped
- Owned as Staff Software Engineer: the architecture and the delivery

</Columns>

## Where it sits

Table View was already my surface before the Staff role. From 2018 I worked in the group that built Wrike Resource, Wrike for Professional Services and the Automation Engine, mostly on Reporting, Table View, Budgeting, time tracking, request forms and automation. The re-architecture is the Staff chapter of that same grid, from July 2022 in Prague.

The measurable part is the one in the CV. 100k rows, render time from 10 seconds to 3, and the performance incidents that had been coming back are gone.
